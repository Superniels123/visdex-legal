Parties we work with

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Listed here are the parties that receive personal data of VISDEX users. Why, on what legal basis and which data a party receives is set out in the privacy policy, in the block that the Purpose column refers to. A processor works on our behalf, only for our purpose and on the basis of a data processing agreement. A party that is an independent controller decides itself what it does with the data, in accordance with its own privacy policy.

If a party is located outside the European Union, the last column shows the safeguard: the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (DPF) if the party participates in it, the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission (SCCs), or a decision of the European Commission that the country provides an adequate level of protection (adequacy decision).
