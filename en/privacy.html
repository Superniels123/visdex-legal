VISDEX Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

In short: VISDEX is a fish logbook app. Your exact location and your spots are private by default, and you choose yourself what others see of your catches. We do not sell your data. You only receive marketing email if you give your consent to it yourself. You can delete your account and your data yourself in the app at any time.

1. Who we are

VISDEX ("we") is established in the Netherlands and registered with the Netherlands Chamber of Commerce (Kamer van Koophandel) under number 86629409. We are the controller of the personal data processed through the VISDEX app. For questions about privacy and to exercise your rights (see 9), email info@visdex.nl.

2. How to read this policy

In 3, each processing activity is set out in a fixed block: what we process, why, on what legal basis, who receives the data and how long we keep it. The legal basis is in each case one of these four from Article 6 of the GDPR:

  • Contract: needed to provide you with the app you use.
  • Consent: only if you gave it yourself. You can always withdraw it again, without consequences for the rest of the app.
  • Legitimate interest: an interest of ours or of other anglers, which is stated in each block. You can object to it (see 9).
  • Legal obligation: if the law requires us to do something.

If a block does not state its own retention period, we keep the data for as long as your account exists. What happens when you delete your account is set out in 8. Under "Who" we name types of parties; which companies they are is set out on the page Parties we work with (see 5).

For an account you only need an email address and a username. You fill in everything else yourself, if you want to. If you do not fill in your date of birth, we treat you as a minor and some features are turned off (see 4).

3.3 What others see of your catches

For each catch you choose one of three visibility settings:

  • Public: the catch appears in the Discover feed, with species, length, photo and the province. If you are 18 or older, a new catch has this setting by default, unless you turn off "New catches public by default".
  • Competitive: the catch counts towards leaderboards and records, without water or province, and does not appear in the Discover feed. Under 18, a new catch has this setting by default.
  • Private: only for you.
What Discover feed: only for signed-in VISDEX users. Your username, profile photo and your catches set to Public. "Show my public catches in the Discover feed" in Settings > Privacy is on by default; if you turn it off, your catches do not appear in the feed. For each catch, you choose with its setting. There, with "New catches public by default", you also choose whether a new catch already appears in the Discover feed when you save it; you still choose for each catch yourself after that. With "All catches at once" you put all your catches in the Discover feed in one go, or take them all out of it. Catches that count in a running competition, giveaway or the Fish of the Week stay as they are when you do this. Sharing publicly is only possible from the age of 18.
Leaderboards: per country, and in the Hall of Fame also a European list with the flag of the country linked to your account. Your username, profile photo and your catches that count. You only appear on them if you turn on "Show me on the VISDEX leaderboard" yourself in Settings > Privacy; it is off by default. The leaderboards can also be viewed without a VISDEX account, for example on visdex.nl.
Fish of the Week: you take part with anglers from your own country.
On the map: if you turn on "Put this catch on the VISDEX map" for a catch, other signed-in VISDEX users see the exact location of that catch on the map, with species, size, date and the name of the water. This is only possible for a catch in the Discover feed and from the age of 18. The pin only appears one day after the catch, and not during a close season for that species. You turn it off again per catch. No one else sees the coordinates of all your other catches and your spots.
WhySharing catches with other anglers and taking part in the leaderboards, if you choose to.
Legal basisContract. You choose yourself what you share, with the settings and switches above.
WhoOther users, and for the leaderboards also visitors without an account. Our hosting service (see 5).
How longFor as long as the catch has that setting. We cannot take back what someone has already seen.

If you share a catch to Instagram Stories, the app passes the image and the public app ID of VISDEX to the Instagram app on your device. VISDEX itself sends nothing to Meta when you do this. The Instagram app belongs to Meta, so what you share reaches Meta that way; what you then post on Instagram is covered by Instagram's terms.

3.6 Weather and water for your catch and your session

WhatThe weather for a catch and at the start of a session, retrieved using the location and the time. Your device sends the location to our own server; the server rounds the coordinates before passing them to a weather service, to within about 10 kilometres for one weather service and to within about 1 kilometre for the other. Older versions of the app still retrieve the hourly weather directly from your device. The app retrieves tides from Rijkswaterstaat (the Dutch public works and water management agency) using only the code of a measuring station, without your location; our server retrieves water conditions from there. The app looks up the name of the water and the province for a location via your device's map service (Apple on iPhone, Google on Android), using the exact coordinates.
WhyContext for your catch and your session.
Legal basisContract.
WhoWeather services, Rijkswaterstaat, Apple or Google (see 5).
How longWe keep the weather with the catch or session, for as long as it exists.

3.25 Video with a catch

WhatA short video you record in the app with a catch (without sound) or choose from your gallery, the time of recording, and still images from that video.
WhyShowing your catch to the people you choose.
WhoThe video is stored with an external video service in the European Union (Frankfurt). It can only be played in the app, using a link that is valid for a few minutes. There is no button to download or forward a video.
How longWe keep a video until you delete it, or until you delete the catch or your account; we then also delete it from the video service. We delete a rejected video after 30 days. A recording that is on your device and does not belong to a saved catch is deleted from your device after 24 hours.

You can record a short video in the app with a catch: up to 15 seconds and without sound. The app only uses your camera for this, not your microphone and not your location.

Video from your gallery. You can also choose an existing video from the gallery on your device, up to 30 seconds long. Such a video does not get the "Live filmed" mark and does not count as proof anywhere. The sound of a video from your gallery is not played anywhere: before sending, the app takes the audio track and the location data out of the table of contents of the file. The app reads the recording date from the file and stores it with the video, so that we can see when it was recorded when we manage the app.

Who sees the video. A video has its own setting, separate from the catch. By default only you and your fishing buddies see it. Public is a choice you make per video. If you are under 18, or your date of birth is not known, your video cannot become public. Please note: your spot may be recognisable in a video.

Review. A video only becomes public after someone at VISDEX has looked at it. Beforehand, we have a few still images from the video checked automatically by the service that also checks catch photos (see 3.18); the video itself is not sent there, and a video you record in the app is never rejected automatically. We do not look at videos that are only for you or your fishing buddies in advance; anyone who may see such a video can report it.

Checking a video from your gallery. Before we accept a video from your gallery, an automated check (see 7) looks at five still images from the video, never at the video itself. The check looks for three things: is there a fish in it, is it a recording of a screen, and is there anything in view that gives away a place, such as a number plate or a house number. If there is no fish in it, or it is a recording of a screen, we do not accept the video; you can then choose another video or film live. In case of doubt the video stays visible only to you and your fishing buddies. We count how many videos were not accepted per reason; these are numbers only.

No reuse. We do not use your video on social media or in advertisements, unless you give separate consent for that video. This also applies to a public video.

VISDEX can be used from the age of 13; in Australia, from the age of 16. If you think we are processing data of a child under 13, email info@visdex.nl and we will delete it. If we do not know your date of birth, we treat you as under 16 for the rules below, and the AI features do not work (see 3.16).

  • Under 18: you cannot set a catch to Public. Only your fishing buddies see your catches in the Discover feed and your profile. You do not appear in the Hall of Fame. You cannot put a catch on the VISDEX map, you do not take part in prize draws, and your photo and name do not appear on our social media. Restrictions apply to contact with unknown adults: you are only in a group chat or the chat of a competition if every adult in it is your fishing buddy. Customer support does not send your messages and photos to the AI service, and you do not receive photos from VISDEX in the chat.

Your photo does not go to the AI service for giveaways and competitions (see 3.17).   • Leaderboard: it is off by default for everyone. If you turn it on yourself, you appear on the leaderboard with your username and your catches that count, also under 18 (see 3.3).

A video of a user under 18, or whose date of birth is not known, cannot become public (see 3.25).

7. Automated decisions

Two checks can automatically decide something about a catch. In a public competition, the check from 3.17 can determine that a catch does not count. The check from 3.18 can remove a catch from the public overviews until someone from VISDEX has looked at it. You see such a decision with the catch, together with the reason, and with the Ask for a review button a person looks at it again. Your catch itself, your logbook and the leaderboards do not change as a result, and a catch is not rejected automatically. We do not create a profile of you for advertising.

8. If you delete your account

If you delete your account in Settings > Delete account > Delete my account, your personal data, your catches, your photos, your locations, your messages and your support conversations are deleted immediately and permanently, as are your contact at the email marketing service and a sign-up for the waiting list or a giveaway on visdex.nl with the same email address. Six things remain:

  • Competitions you created yourself and that others took part in, because otherwise we would also erase their results. You then appear as "Deleted user". If your competition had no other participants, it disappears completely.
  • Proof that the deletion was carried out: only an encrypted code that cannot be reversed, and the time, without name or email address.
  • The log of the VISDEX admin team's decisions: which action was taken on an account ID, with the explanation and the details of that action, without date of birth. We need this to be able to look into abuse and child safety (see 3.22).
  • In the case of a report of abuse, the snapshot of the reported conversation, with the names and messages, until twelve months after the report has been dealt with (see 3.22).
  • Payment details of a subscription (amount, product, store and date), without a link to your account, because the law requires us to keep them (see 3.10).
  • Aggregated figures that cannot be traced back to you (see 3.8).

For catch videos, the periods under "Video with a catch" in 3.25 apply.
