VISDEX Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

In short: VISDEX is a fish logbook app. Your catches and locations are private by default. You only share what you choose to share. We never sell your data. We only send you marketing email if you have given your consent, and you can withdraw it at any time. You can delete your account and all your data completely at any time from within the app.

1. Who are we?

VISDEX ("we", "us", "VISDEX") is established in the Netherlands and registered with the Netherlands Chamber of Commerce (Kamer van Koophandel) under number 86629409. We are the controller of the personal data processed through the VISDEX app.

For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, you can contact us at info@visdex.nl.

3. How do we use your data?

  • Making the app work – logging your catches, showing your collection and the map, chatting and sharing with fishing buddies.
  • Sharing on your privacy terms – you decide per catch, spot or fishing buddy what is visible. Locations are never shared automatically.
  • Keeping the community safe – we process reports and blocks to prevent abuse.
  • Improving – we use feedback you send us and anonymised error reports to make the app better.

4. Public leaderboard, Discover feed and communication

VISDEX has a national leaderboard and a Discover feed. In the Discover feed, your username, avatar and public catches (species, length and photo), together with the province of the catch, are visible to signed-in VISDEX users. On the leaderboards, signed-in users see your name, username, avatar and score, and in the app also your catches that are not set to private, such as your biggest fish per species. The leaderboards per discipline (predator, carp, coarse and sea fishing) are public: your name, username, avatar and score can be retrieved there without a VISDEX account as well, and visdex.nl shows your name and score from them. Without an account, these leaderboards show anglers from the Netherlands. Minors appear anonymously on the leaderboards (see 13).

The leaderboard is off by default: you only appear on it once you choose to, with "Show me on the VISDEX leaderboard" under Settings > Privacy, or when the app asks you, for example after your second catch. The Discover feed is on by default for your public catches; you turn it off with "Show my public catches in the Discover feed" under Settings > Privacy, or per catch with the Discover toggle on the catch screen. You can change both at any time.

Your precise location and spot are private by default. On the leaderboards and in the Discover feed, only the province of a catch is visible.

There is one exception, and you choose it yourself per catch: if you turn on "Put this catch on the VISDEX map" for a public catch with a photo, other signed-in VISDEX users will see that catch on the national map at the place where you caught it, together with the name of the water. The pin only appears 24 hours after the catch (or after logging, if you log it later), and for an account in the Netherlands not during a close season for that species. This option is off by default and is only available from the age of 18, if you have entered your date of birth; so never for minors. You can turn it off again per catch. Coordinates of catches without a pin, your saved spots and your private notes always stay private.

VISDEX may also use a publicly shared catch in our own community content, including posts on social media (such as Instagram), for example a weekly "catch of the week" round-up.

Legal basis: the leaderboard, the Discover feed and the pin on the map are part of the app, and for those the legal basis is the contract. For our own community content it is legitimate interest, namely showing what is caught with VISDEX.

5. Public catches and improving the app

We use catches that you have made public yourself, combined and without your name, to improve the app. For example, we look at how well our estimates of species, length and weight match what anglers actually enter, so that those estimates get better. The legal basis is legitimate interest, namely better estimates in the app.

If you don't want that, set a catch to private, or turn off the Discover toggle on that catch. We never use private catches for this.

6. Email and consent to marketing

We send three kinds of email, and they are separate from each other:

  • Email that belongs to your account – for example resetting your password or confirming your email address. You always receive these; without these messages your account doesn't work.
  • The monthly statistics email – a look back at your own fishing month, with its own separate switch in the app. This is not marketing and is separate from the consent below.
  • Marketing email – only if you have given your consent.

Legal basis: for email that belongs to your account and for the statistics email, it is the contract, because they are part of the app. For marketing email, it is your consent.

What you consent to is stated word for word next to the question: giveaways, exclusive offers from tackle shops and partners, and fishing weather tips. We don't tick anything for you, and the choice is never a condition for using the app. How often we send such email is not fixed.

To be able to show that your consent was genuinely given, we record when you consented, on which screen, and which text you saw at the time. If that text changes in substance, that counts as a new consent and we ask you again.

You can withdraw your consent at any time, via the unsubscribe link at the bottom of every marketing email or via Settings in the app. From that moment on, we no longer send you marketing email. Your email address then stays with that service as an unsubscribed contact until you delete your account.

To send it, we work with an external email marketing service. Two things you should know about this:

  • If you do not give consent, we send nothing about you to that service. If you created an account before 31 August 2026, your email address may still be held there as an administrative contact, so that we know who already has an account; no email at all is sent to such a contact. Only for those contacts from before 31 August 2026 is the legal basis legitimate interest, namely exactly that: knowing who already has an account.
  • If you do give consent, we also share a limited amount of data with that service to personalise the email: your display name, how many catches you have logged, how many different species, your most-caught species, how many achievements you have earned and your invite link. We do not share catch locations, spots, photos, chat messages or notes.

If you delete your account, your contact at that service is deleted too.

Email from a promotion partner. For some competitions and giveaways we ask separately whether a partner, for example Wesdijk Hengelsport, may email you about offers. That question is separate from your consent to our own marketing email, and in the app the box is unticked by default. If you give that consent for a competition in the app, the partner receives your email address, your display name and your username from us. If you give it for a giveaway on our website, we only add your email address to the partner's mailing list at its email service (MailCamp). The legal basis is your consent. You can withdraw it in the app via Settings > Notifications and email, or by emailing info@visdex.nl; we then no longer pass on your data. If the partner has already received your data, you unsubscribe with the partner directly, via the unsubscribe link in its email.

7. AI features

VISDEX has a number of features that use the AI service of Anthropic (Claude), a provider in the United States. There are three kinds of processing: a suggestion for the fish species and length from your photo, advice and overviews based on the data in your fish logbook, and a fishing plan for a photo of the water (Waterscan). They sit behind a single switch, which is off by default: Settings > Logging catches > AI features. The legal basis is your consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), and you can withdraw it there at any time, without consequences for the rest of the app.

a. Species and length from your photo

The app can suggest the fish species and length from the photo of your catch. If the switch is on, Anthropic processes the photo of that particular catch to make that suggestion. With VISDEX Pro this happens automatically as soon as you add a photo while logging; in the trial version only when you ask for it. Before the photo leaves your device, it is reduced in size and the hidden data is removed, including the GPS location your camera writes into the file.

If you ask for a length estimate, we also send which species you chose and what is lying in the photo as a reference, for example the VISDEX reference ball. Nothing else is sent: no name, no location, no other catch data.

We do not keep the photo, but we do keep what the app suggested and what you finally made of it, linked to your account. That way we can see where the estimate is off and improve it.

b. Advice and overviews based on your catch data

If you ask for a species hunt plan, your fishing patterns or session advice, or when the app creates your weekly review, data from your own fish logbook goes to Anthropic, which turns it into text. The app creates the weekly review automatically when you open your Collection. What is sent differs per feature, but it comes down to: the date and time of a catch, the fish species, the length and weight, the bait and technique, the name of the water, and the weather data belonging to that catch. For session advice, the province of the water, the weather forecast and what you fill in on the form yourself, such as your bait preference, are sent as well. For your fishing patterns and your weekly review, no individual catches are sent, only totals, for example how many catches per month and which bait works for you most often.

In addition, figures about the community as a whole are sent, such as how much of a species is caught on a water. No names are attached.

What is never sent: your name, your email address, your photos, your notes, your chat messages, and your coordinates or spots. Waters are only sent by name, never as a place on the map.

We keep your hunt plan and your fishing patterns so that you can open them again without a new request. We don't keep the session advice and the weekly review; the weekly review stays on your device only. For each use we do record that you used the feature and when, to prevent abuse and unnecessary costs. The legal basis for this is legitimate interest, namely exactly that.

c. A fishing plan for a photo of the water (Waterscan)

With Waterscan you take a photo with your camera of the water where you are standing. The app suggests a short fishing plan for it: where in the photo you can fish, for which species, and with which bait and technique. If the switch is on, that photo goes, only at your request, to Anthropic. Before the photo leaves your device, it is reduced in size and saved as a new file, without the hidden data such as the GPS location.

Sent with the photo: the date, time and season, the weather at that moment, the name and type of the water according to the map, which species you yourself caught earlier within 500 metres and how often, and which species are open today. Your coordinates do not go to the AI service: the app only uses them to look up the water and the weather, through our own server and the weather service (see 9). Your name, your email address and your notes are not sent. Waterscan follows the same rule as the other AI features: under 16 you cannot turn the switch on (see 13).

We do not keep the photo or the fishing plan. For each use we record that you used Waterscan and when, with technical details of the request such as the cost, to prevent abuse and unnecessary costs. The legal basis for this is legitimate interest, namely exactly that.

For all three

Your data and your photos are not used to train AI models. If you turn the switch off, nothing leaves your device for these features any more and they stop working. As long as you haven't turned it on yourself, nothing happens at all.

8. Advertising measurement

We advertise to reach new anglers, and we want to know whether that works. To do this, the app sends a small number of technical events to the advertising platform we advertise on, such as "app installed" and "app opened", together with general device data.

Exactly what is sent differs per platform:

  • On iPhone and iPad we do not ask for permission to track, so your advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used. Measurement runs exclusively through Apple's aggregated method (SKAdNetwork), which cannot be traced back to you.
  • On Android, from version 1.0.12 onwards we no longer send your advertising identifier (GAID), and the app no longer forwards events automatically. If you use an older version, the GAID may still be sent until you update the app; you can also reset or delete it yourself in the Android settings, under the privacy or ads settings.

What is never sent: your catches, your locations or spots, your photos, your chat messages and your profile data.

9. Who do we share data with?

We never sell your data. We only share data with parties that are needed to make the app work (processors), on the basis of a data processing agreement:

  • Supabase – our database and storage service (hosting your data, in the EU region where possible).
  • Weather service – for retrieving weather information for a catch and at the start of a session.
  • Apple / Google – for delivering the app through their app stores and (if turned on) push notifications.
  • Meta (Facebook) – for measuring app install campaigns. The Meta SDK in the app sends technical events (such as "app installed" and "app opened") and general device data to Meta for this purpose. On Android, the advertising identifier (GAID) is no longer sent from version 1.0.12 onwards; with older versions this may still happen until you update the app. On iOS we do not ask for tracking permission, so your advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used; install measurement there runs exclusively through Apple's aggregated SKAdNetwork, which cannot be traced back to you. Catches, locations or profile data are never shared with Meta.
  • Anthropic (Claude) – the AI service for the species and length suggestion from a catch photo, for the fishing plan for a photo of the water (Waterscan), and for creating your hunt plan, your fishing patterns, your session advice and your weekly review from the data in your fish logbook, only if you have turned on the AI features yourself (see 7).
  • Subscription management – for handling and keeping track of VISDEX Pro: whether your subscription is running, when it renews and when it ends. You make the purchase itself with Apple or Google; this service keeps track for us of what follows from it.
  • Email marketing – for sending our email and managing sign-ups and unsubscribes (see 6).
  • Advertising measurement – for measuring whether our ads work (see 8).
  • App updates and push messages – for delivering app updates and push messages. This involves processing your IP address and a technical device code.
  • Maps – for showing the map in the app. This involves processing your IP address and the map area you are viewing, not your catches.
  • Transactional email – for sending email that belongs to your account, such as password resets.

Data outside the European Union. Some of these parties are based in the United States or process data there, for example Anthropic (the AI features) and the providers of advertising measurement, email marketing, subscription management, push messages, app updates and maps. We make sure there is a valid legal basis for this: the EU-US Data Privacy Framework where the party participates in it, and otherwise the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission. If you want to know which safeguard applies to which party, email info@visdex.nl.

We name categories here rather than every brand name, because suppliers can change. If you want to know exactly which parties we currently work with, email info@visdex.nl and we will send you the current list.

You also share data with other users yourself when you choose to (fishing buddies, chats, public catches), and with your consent we pass data on to a promotion partner (see 6).

Searching for a place. If you search in the app for a place, street or water, for example on the map or when choosing a location, what you type goes directly from your device to Photon, a public search service from komoot based on OpenStreetMap. komoot also sees your IP address. Your account, your name and your own location are not sent.

10. How long do we keep your data?

We keep your data for as long as you have an account. For some data a shorter period or a rule of its own applies:

  • Technical logs of our hosting, with your IP address: 7 days at most. The legal basis is legitimate interest, namely securing the app and fixing outages.
  • Proof of your consent to marketing email and to the AI features: for as long as that consent applies. If you withdraw it, we only keep the fact that you did so and when, until you delete your account.
  • The record of your use of the AI features (that and when, see 7): for as long as your account exists.
  • Usage data (see 2): we delete part of it after 90 or 120 days, and keep the rest for as long as your account exists.
  • Your sign-up for a giveaway through our website: six months after you signed up.

If you delete your account, your personal data, your catches, your photos, your locations and your messages are deleted immediately and permanently, and your contact at the email service is deleted as well.

Five things remain after that, and it is only fair to say so:

  • Competitions you created yourself and that others took part in. We don't delete these, because that would also wipe the results of all the other participants. Your name is removed: you then appear as "Deleted user". If your competition had no other participants, it is deleted completely.
  • Proof that the deletion was carried out. We only keep an encrypted code that cannot be reversed, and the time. It contains no name, email address or any other data of yours. This is needed to be able to show that we actually carried out a deletion request.
  • Anonymised, aggregated figures that cannot be traced back to you, for example regional catch statistics.
  • Reports of abuse and our log of moderation decisions. We keep these so that we can show why we stepped in; the legal basis is legitimate interest, namely preventing abuse and being able to account for it. We delete the evidence attached to a report, with the names and messages the report is about, twelve months after the report has been dealt with. If a case is running with an outside authority, that period only starts once that case is closed.
  • Payment details of a subscription (amount, product, store and date), without any link to your account. We have to keep these for our accounts; the legal basis is a legal obligation.

11. Your rights (GDPR)

You have the right to:

  • Access – ask which data we hold about you.
  • Rectification – have incorrect data corrected (you can edit your profile and catches yourself in the app).
  • Erasure – delete your account and all your data. You can do this directly in the app via Settings → Delete account.
  • Data portability – request an export of your data (partly available in the app via data export).
  • Objection / withdrawing consent – turn sharing off again or object.

You can withdraw all the consents you give in the app at any time, without giving a reason and without consequences for the rest of the app:

  • Marketing email – via the unsubscribe link at the bottom of every marketing email, or via Settings in the app. You will then no longer receive marketing email.
  • AI features – via Settings > Logging catches > AI features in the app. After that, no photo and no catch data leaves your device for these features any more.
  • Email from a promotion partner – via Settings > Notifications and email in the app, or by emailing info@visdex.nl (see 6).

Send your request to info@visdex.nl. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens (the Dutch Data Protection Authority).

12. Security

We protect your data with appropriate technical measures, including encrypted connections and access rules at database level (row-level security), so that only you, and the people you choose to share with, can access your data. Sensitive data such as exact locations is stored separately and with stricter protection, and is only visible to others if you choose so yourself (for example by putting a catch on the national map or by sharing a spot).

13. Children

VISDEX is for everyone aged 13 and over. Under 16, we send no marketing email and you cannot turn on the AI features (see 7).

Minors (under 18) have extra protection:

  • No pin on the map – a minor cannot put a catch on the national map (see 4).
  • Limited contact with adults – adults cannot send a minor a fishing buddy request, in a group chat with a minor there are only adults who are already that minor's fishing buddies, and spots and catch locations cannot be shared between minors and adults. Exception: in the chat of a private competition, all participants of that competition take part.
  • Anonymous on the leaderboards – on the national leaderboards a minor appears as "Visser" (angler), without a username or profile photo.
  • Sponsor giveaways only with a parent's consent – a minor can only take part in a sponsor's giveaway if a parent or guardian agrees.

If you think we are processing data of a child under 13, email info@visdex.nl and we will delete it.
