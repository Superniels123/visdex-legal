1. About VISDEX

VISDEX is a fish logbook and community app, offered by VISDEX (KvK 86629409). With VISDEX you log catches, build a collection, share with fishing buddies and take part in fishing competitions.

Real rewards for challenges. In the app you can do challenges. As long as we offer them, you can also earn Pro days or a Pro gift with a challenge, in every country, and in the Netherlands also a chance at the prize of the month. Every level earns XP; on top of that, per level:

  • Bronze – XP only.
  • Silver – 7 days of VISDEX Pro. If you already have a paid Pro subscription, or Pro as a founding member, you get a Pro gift for a fishing buddy instead: a code for 30 days of VISDEX Pro.
  • Gold in the monthly challenge and in the final challenge of an expedition – in the Netherlands only: a chance at the prize of the month. That chance only comes through the prize draw of the monthly giveaway, under its Dutch terms: your account country is the Netherlands, you are 18 or older, and the catches you reached gold with are approved (with a photo and verified). If you never chose your country yourself, first choose the Netherlands as your country in your settings; otherwise you do not get this chance. There is not a prize every month. Which prize there is that month, and who the sponsor of that month is, you can see in the app. Outside the Netherlands there is no draw and no physical prize.

Pro days. Pro days from a challenge are not a subscription: you pay nothing, nothing renews automatically and there is nothing to cancel. When they end, you simply have a free account again. If your welcome Pro is still running, or you still have Pro days from before, the 7 days are added after them. Pro days have no monetary value: you cannot exchange them for money or transfer them to someone else.

Pro gift. A Pro gift is one code for 30 days of VISDEX Pro, which can be redeemed once. Only another account that has no active Pro at that moment can redeem it; a welcome Pro that is still running does not count as active Pro here. If you redeem a code during your welcome Pro, the 30 days are added after it ends. You can never redeem your own code yourself. The code is valid for 90 days after it is issued; after that it expires, without compensation. You may give the code to one person, but you may not resell it. A Pro gift has no monetary value either, and for those 30 days too you pay nothing, nothing renews and there is nothing to cancel.

Limits. Per account you get at most one reward with Pro days per 30 days, and at most one Pro gift per calendar month. Only a challenge whose completion we establish on our own server earns a real reward. If a challenge is about catches, only approved catches (with a photo and verified) count for silver and gold. A catch in a close season that we know for your country never counts, and in the Netherlands neither does a catch below the minimum size.

No right to a reward. We may limit the number of rewards with Pro days per week. If that weekly budget has run out, you get the usual XP at silver and no Pro days. The Pro gift does not fall under that weekly budget; only the limit of one per calendar month applies to it. You have no right to a reward. We can also stop the real rewards altogether; the XP then simply stays. Pro days you have already received and codes that have already been issued remain valid, except in case of abuse. While the rewards are paused, a code cannot be redeemed for a while; the code itself stays valid until it expires.

Abuse. If we see abuse, such as fake catches, multiple accounts or trading in codes, we may refuse or withdraw a reward. This also applies to Pro days that are already running and to codes that have already been issued.

Under 18. You can also get Pro days and a Pro gift if you are under 18; no money and no address are involved. You can only take part in the prize draw from the age of 18, and you only get a physical prize from the age of 18 as well.
